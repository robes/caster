Your model card (Mitchell et al., 2019): the human-facing companion to the

datasets, metrics, provenance) are not written here: `caster package` puts them

Releasing several models? Copy this file to MODEL_CARD.<model_id>.md for each
(<model_id> as in its release config), or keep one card for all of them.
